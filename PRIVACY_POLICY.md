# Privacy Policy for GeoFluent - Geometry Calculator

**Last Updated:** October 17, 2026

GeoFluent - Geometry Calculator ("GeoFluent", "the Application", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the information we collect and process when you use our application on Windows 10 and Windows 11.

---

### 1. Zero Data Collection Policy
GeoFluent is an open-source, offline-first mathematical geometry application. We believe in absolute user privacy:
- **No Personal Information**: We do not collect, request, log, or store your name, email address, physical location, IP address, device identifiers, or any other personally identifiable information (PII).
- **No Telemetry or Analytics**: The application contains no tracking code, telemetry libraries, analytics engines, advertising SDKs, or third-party monitoring services.
- **No Network Transmission**: Calculations, geometrical dimensions, unit conversions, and formula derivations are computed entirely offline on your local computer CPU/GPU. The application does not send your data to external servers or cloud services.

---

### 2. Local Storage
GeoFluent saves non-personal preferences locally on your device solely to enhance your user experience:
- **Calculation History**: Saved geometric calculations are stored exclusively within your local application storage so you can review or restore past calculations.
- **Application Settings**: Display theme preferences (Light, Dark, or System) and decimal precision settings are saved locally on your device.
- **Data Deletion**: You can clear your saved calculation history at any time directly within the application settings, or by resetting the application through Windows Settings.

---

### 3. Children's Privacy
GeoFluent is designed for students, educators, engineers, and general mathematics enthusiasts of all ages. Because GeoFluent does not collect any personal information whatsoever, it complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar global privacy regulations.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services
GeoFluent does not integrate with, transmit data to, or share data with any third-party marketing, analytics, or advertising platforms.
